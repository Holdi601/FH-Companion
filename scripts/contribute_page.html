Contribute a scan

One scan run on your machine, and its laps join the shared records. Back to the ratings

1. Get the tool

A folder of readable scripts, not an .exe. start.cmd finds Python or installs it, sets up the environment, and starts.


It runs entirely on your machine: it reads the game screen and sends key presses to the game. Nothing leaves your computer until you upload the package below. The tool is not public — it drives someone else's game by remote control, so it goes out to people who were given the password, and the same lockout applies: ten wrong ones and this machine waits 24 hours.

2. Scan

  1. Double-click start.cmd.
  2. Pick a category and class. Only categories the catalogue knows are allowed — the tool refuses the rest rather than filing laps under the wrong category.
  3. Leave the game open and the machine alone: the tool drives the menus itself.
  4. When it finishes, a .zip is waiting in out\.

3. Upload the package

Drop the ZIP here — or


Two things are checked, and they are deliberately separate: the two signatures inside the package, which only someone with their own key can produce, and the typed password, which ships with nothing. A forwarded ZIP on its own is therefore not enough. After ten wrong passwords this machine is locked out for 24 hours — including for the right one. A rejected package changes nothing in the records.
