Get it

Download for Windows

A folder, not an installer. Unpack it anywhere and double-click START.cmd — nothing has to be installed, and it needs no internet connection to work: the whole lap record ships inside the package.

What it does

  • Two panels over the game. One says which of the three offered routes suits your car; the other says which cars are worth driving in this class. Both are worked out from the laps of real players in this record, not from opinions.
  • Grip haptics. How much grip each tyre has left becomes vibration — on the side the car is losing. Steam Controller (four actuators), DualSense including the adaptive triggers, Xbox, PlayStation and 8BitDo pads.
  • A blueprint editor. Telemetry to vibration is a node graph, not a fixed program. Save and load your own as .fhgraph.json.

The keys

F8The routes on offer, best first. Press again to send it away.
F6Your car: where it stands in each class and category.
F7Switch between points and the sum of the lap times.
F9Pin the panel that is up, so it stays past its 30 seconds.
Page Up / DownWalk the rest of the field — a ranking runs hundreds of cars deep, and a panel shows what fits.

One setting in the game

Forza sends its telemetry to exactly one place, and it is off by default: Settings → HUD and Gameplay, at the bottom.

Data Out          On
Data Out IP       127.0.0.1
Data Out Port     5300
Data Out Format   Dash   (or Sled)

Without it the haptics stay silent and every number reads 0 — the game is sending nothing. The panels do not need it; they read the screen.

What it does not do

It reads your screen and your own telemetry, and it draws over the game. It does not send anything anywhere, does not touch the game's files, and stays idle while Forza is not running. Sharing scans is a separate tool and a separate decision: contribute a scan.
